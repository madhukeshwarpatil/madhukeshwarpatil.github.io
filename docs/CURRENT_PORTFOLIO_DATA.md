- **Strong, quantified content.** Metrics (9M users, 90 days, 8K req/s, 60M users, MTTR reduction) are exactly what recruiters scan for. The narrative arc (SaaS → fintech reliability → OTT architecture lead) is clear.
- **Clear positioning.** Title, availability, location, and visa status are all stated up front — this answers a recruiter's first three questions immediately.
- **Complete contact info** and a resume download.

### Weaknesses / opportunities
- **Visually undifferentiated.** A static text page does not signal seniority or craft; it reads like a hosted resume rather than a portfolio. For a Technical Lead competing in the EU market, a memorable, polished site is itself proof of quality standards.
- **No visual hierarchy for metrics.** The strongest content (the big numbers) is buried in bullet lists instead of being the visual centerpiece.
- **No storytelling.** The three-act career story (build → operate at scale → architect from zero) isn't staged; everything has equal weight.
- **No motion, no depth, no brand.** No visual identity connecting his domain (cloud infrastructure, observability, data flow) to the presentation.
- **Skills are flat comma lists** — hard to scan, no grouping emphasis or proficiency signal.
- **Likely weak SEO/social sharing** — no evidence of OG tags or rich meta.
